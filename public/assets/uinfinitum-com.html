Week 01 — Immersion
Environment Access + Dataset Orientation
You receive your simulation environment and dataset. The first week is pure exploration. Understand the structure, identify the problems, document your initial observations in the built-in research log.
Week 02 — Analysis
Deep Work + Structured Problem Solving
Core work phase. You apply your domain knowledge to the dataset. The environment tracks every meaningful action. Your educator monitors progress through the Syndicate dashboard in real time.
Week 03 — Output
Synthesis + Graded Submission
Final week. You produce a structured output — an analysis, a solution, a model. The system auto-generates a performance report for your institution. You keep a copy permanently.
